ABOUT JOZ

Bringing structure to academic teamwork.

Successful academic projects require more than putting students together. They require balanced teams, clear responsibilities and measurable contributions.

What is JOZ?

JOZ is a Smart Academic Team Formation and Contribution Management System designed to support university-based collaborative projects.

It helps academic administrators organize team formation, enables lecturers to manage academic assignments and project supervision, and gives students a structured environment to collaborate and demonstrate their contributions.

Balanced Teams

Form teams by considering student capabilities and academic compatibility.

Transparent Contributions

Organize tasks and monitor individual contributions throughout projects.

PLATFORM CAPABILITIES

Everything needed for better teamwork.

A connected academic workflow from team formation to project completion.

Smart Team Formation

Generate balanced academic teams using technical skills, academic performance, interests, preferences, soft skills and problem-solving ability.

Project Management

Organize academic assignments, project proposals, submission deadlines and lecturer supervision.

Task Management

Assign project responsibilities, organize tasks and follow their completion throughout the project.

Contribution Monitoring

Track individual participation and support transparent contribution evaluation.

Notifications

Keep students and lecturers informed about important academic activities and deadlines.

Reports and Analytics

Provide academic oversight through organized reports, project progress and contribution information.

ACADEMIC WORKFLOW

From team formation to project completion.

JOZ connects the main stages of collaborative academic project management.

01

Set Requirements

Academic administrators organize eligible students and team formation requirements.

02

Form Teams

The system generates balanced teams using approved student matching criteria.

03

Manage Projects

Students submit project proposals while lecturers supervise and evaluate them.

04

Monitor Contributions

Teams manage tasks and lecturers monitor progress and individual contributions.

ONE PLATFORM, THREE ROLES

Designed for the entire academic community.

Each role has dedicated tools and responsibilities within the JOZ platform.

Students

Manage academic profiles, provide matching information, collaborate with assigned teams, manage tasks and track contributions.

Lecturers

Manage courses and assignments, supervise project proposals, monitor teams, evaluate tasks and review contributions.

Administrators

Manage accounts, student eligibility, team formation, team administration and overall system oversight.
